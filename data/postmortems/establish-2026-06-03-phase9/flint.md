## 4. Recommendations for Phase 10

1. **Phase 10.J — `bot/lib/runtime/nav-brief.js`: ALWAYS emit `terrain_kind`, including `flat` and `unknown`.** Flint's run-6 is the strongest single piece of evidence we have. A worker standing on cleaned spawn grass at Y=96 confabulated "I'm underground at Y=96, surface is Y=100-105" and burned the rest of the run trying to pillar up. Noise-reduction is the wrong trade when the missing signal is exactly the one preventing a 45-minute deadlock. Cost is ~10 chars/round per worker; benefit is killing Pattern E at the root.
2. **SOUL bullet for all workers: "If your Y is lower than a remembered pad/anchor Y, do NOT assume you're underground — check `terrain.kind` in your nav-brief first. The base is on a plateau; spawn is on grass at lower elevation. Both are surface."** This is a defensive complement to 10.J — even after the renderer is fixed, the run-5/run-6 pattern of cross-referencing chat-history Y-values needs to be inverted.
3. **Protocol-violation false-abandonment (third recurrence): bump SUPPLY/EXPLORE default `max_retries` to 2, OR inject a `kanban_complete --note "round budget exhausted, see chat for state"` finalizer hook into the worker session cleanup slot.** t_2ae214c1 was a one-shot hermes interrupt mid-API-call; the bot was alive, the kernel killed the card. Same shape as run-5's t_a8650983. This pattern has now cost three explore cards across three runs.
4. **Steward whisper effectiveness audit.** Steward issued 3+ rescue whispers ("PHYSICALLY_STUCK at (14,102,8)", "try a different approach", and a `dig_area 5 5 5 escape + mc move 100 102 100` compound). Flint's bot received them as Minecraft chat lines but the worker process was either kanban_held or had no reading-comprehension path back to its own SCOUT card. Either chat-whispers need to land in the worker's next-round prompt directly, or rescue should be a re-spawn with the card body edited to include the rescue script (which is what Steward did manually, and it still didn't work). Per the SCOUT card's run_id=9 reclaim at 09:53, three respawn cycles produced no behavioural change — the bot kept reaching the same dead-end on each restart.
5. **(16,102,54) door tar-pit — Phase 9 carryover.** Run-5 rec #1 (alt-drop-coord for behind-door chests, or `mc through --no_close`) was not addressed in Phase 9 and bit Flint again in run-6 even though her destination was nowhere near the shelter. The pathfinder still routes through this door as a shortcut. Either demolish/edit the shelter at world-prep time, or have `mc through` cost-blacklist coords with prior `Opened … but bot stalled` failures within the session.
