## Recommendations for Phase 10

1. **Stabilize multi-target SCOUT/verify cards (top priority).** Either (a) extend PR-B's escalate guard to `[SCOUT]` cards whose body has **≥3 coord targets AND zero `mc` verb lines** — t_1d175784 fits this signature exactly — and have Steward re-decompose into three single-target `mc goto_mark` + `mc scene` + `mc chat_to steward` triplets; or (b) add a worker-side "verify protocol" skill that automates the loop on prose targets. Without one of these, every verify-style card is a 15-min crash-loop liability.
2. **Widen PR-H's spawn-cleanup rect to cover Z=55–70.** The residual hut at ~(13,101,62) is the single biggest run-6 derail for Gatherer (and a Pattern-D source for the whole fleet). The cleanup script already knows the coord — extend the rect or whitelist-clear the door/glass_pane blocks.
3. **Phase 10.J: surface flat terrain in nav-brief.** The renderer skip-flat gap (shared with Flint) blinded her to "you're inside a structure" before she dropped into the hut. Even a single `terrain_kind=flat structure_overlap=cobblestone_shelter` hint at observe-time would let her route around it.
4. **Continue PR-C monitoring.** 1× `15000ms` cap vs 6× `8000ms` in run-5 is a real win, but watch for the cap migrating to 15s as the new normal in longer-range pad surveys; the right next move (if it recurs) is **not** a further cap bump — it's the chunk-visibility ticket #41.
