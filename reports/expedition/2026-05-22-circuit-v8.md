## What didn't work

### A. Disembark ↔ sail recursion loop (PRE-EXISTING, **NOT** introduced by this plan)

At 05:32:56 the bot log started spamming `[disembark] in open water — auto-sailing to shore at 341,63,-544 (distance 2.24)` every 1.5s. **900 occurrences** over the remainder of the run.

Root cause (code path):
- `sail()` stall-loop fallback (`bot/lib/actions/water.js:1057-1059`) calls `ACTIONS.disembark({})` when stalled and detour budget exhausted.
- `disembark()` (line ~1144) sees `b.vehicle` set with the boat in open water, calls `ACTIONS.sail()` to navigate to shore.
- `sail()` stalls again → calls `disembark()` again → loop.

Neither side observes a cooldown. The recursion ran continuously even though Steve was on land (364, 61, -607) mining cobblestone — `b.vehicle` had a stale reference to a far-away boat at (340.x, 62, -540) that wasn't actually under Steve.

This is independent of all Phase 1 changes (`useEntity` patch, `lookAt`, force-kill). Force-kill never engages here because the loop runs with `emergency=false` (sail's fallback doesn't pass emergency).

### B. Mining ergonomics — Steve dug himself into an unrecoverable hole

Steve hit the cobblestone outcrop and ran through ~50 `mc dig` / `mc place` / `mc goto_near` calls trying to extract ore. Most returned `[error]` — likely "out of reach" / no LOS. The agent looped between dig + place + scene + status without making progress. Final position (366, 65, -599) is 2 blocks up from where he started mining, holding cobblestone. He **didn't reach W2 (iron_ore)**.

This is the task #34 backlog (workstreams 2-4) — stance selection, auto-clear obstructions, strategic block placement. Not in this plan's scope but the gap is the dominant blocker.
